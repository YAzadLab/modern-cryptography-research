# Research Plan

## Research Question

Why is modern cryptography fundamentally a mathematical problem?

## Aim

To investigate the mathematical principles underlying modern cryptographic
systems and determine why mathematical problems form the basis of their
security.

## Objectives

1. Investigate the mathematical foundations of cryptography.
2. Explore modular arithmetic and number theory.
3. Investigate RSA and its mathematical basis.
4. Investigate Diffie-Hellman and the discrete logarithm problem.
5. Investigate elliptic curve cryptography.
6. Examine the relationship between mathematical difficulty and computational complexity.
7. Implement selected cryptographic algorithms in Python.
8. Attempt to attack simplified cryptographic systems.
9. Investigate the effect of quantum computing on modern cryptography.
10. Examine post-quantum cryptographic approaches.

## Methodology

I will combine mathematical research, programming implementations,
computational experiments and analysis of the results.

## Initial Hypothesis

Modern cryptography is fundamentally mathematical because its security
depends on mathematical problems that are easy to compute in one direction
but computationally difficult to reverse.
